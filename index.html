Uganda

Uganda is a landlocked country located in East Africa, often referred to as the “Pearl of Africa” because of its natural beauty, diverse wildlife, and rich culture. It is home to a wide variety of plant and animal species, including the famous mountain gorillas found in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Uganda is also known for its beautiful landscapes such as Lake Victoria, the largest lake in Africa, and the source of the River Nile. The country has a warm climate, fertile land, and over 50 different ethnic groups, making it one of the most culturally diverse nations in Africa.
